The same relationships, in words

Bennets of Longbourn

Mr. and Mrs. Bennet; five daughters, no son, so the estate is entailed away.

  • Jane (eldest) marries Charles Bingley
  • Elizabeth marries Fitzwilliam Darcy
  • Mary and Catherine (Kitty) stay unmarried
  • Lydia (youngest) elopes with and marries George Wickham
  • Mr. Collins is Mr. Bennet's cousin and heir under the entail; their fathers had quarrelled

Gardiners of Meryton

Mrs. Bennet's family. Her father was an attorney in Meryton.

  • Mrs. Philips, her sister, married Mr. Philips, her father's clerk and successor
  • Mr. Gardiner, her brother, in trade in London, married to Mrs. Gardiner
  • The Gardiners have four children: two girls, two boys
  • Mr. and Mrs. Gardiner are the aunt and uncle who take Elizabeth to Pemberley and later settle Lydia's marriage

Lucases of Lucas Lodge

Neighbours of the Bennets; Sir William was knighted after a career in trade.

  • Charlotte, the eldest, marries Mr. Collins
  • Maria, a younger daughter, visits Hunsford with Elizabeth
  • Several younger children, one boy who speaks

Bingleys

Northern family, fortune made in trade by the late father.

  • Charles, the only son, leases Netherfield and marries Jane
  • Louisa, married to Mr. Hurst
  • Caroline, unmarried, hopes to marry Darcy

Darcys of Pemberley

The late Mr. Darcy married Lady Anne Fitzwilliam, daughter of an earl.

  • Fitzwilliam Darcy, their son, marries Elizabeth
  • Georgiana, their daughter, is the ward of Darcy and Colonel Fitzwilliam
  • George Wickham is the son of the late Mr. Darcy's steward and the late Mr. Darcy's godson; he was raised alongside Darcy and tried to elope with Georgiana

Fitzwilliams and de Bourghs

Lady Anne, Lady Catherine and Lord —— (the Earl) are siblings.

  • Lady Catherine married Sir Lewis de Bourgh; their daughter Anne is heiress of Rosings and was intended for Darcy
  • Colonel Fitzwilliam is the Earl's younger son, so Darcy's and Anne's first cousin
  • Lady Catherine is aunt to Darcy, Georgiana and the Colonel, and patroness to Mr. Collins

Left out on purpose: Mrs. Long and her two nieces, Colonel and Mrs. Forster, the Harringtons and Miss King. They are named kin to each other but tie to no one in the main cast. The earl one generation above Lady Anne and Lady Catherine is never named; the box stands in for whoever produced three siblings with the Fitzwilliam name.
